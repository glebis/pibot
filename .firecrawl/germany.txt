FREQUENTLY 
—ASKED
 QUESTIONS
What is Antler? Is Antler an accelerator or incubator?
Not quite, we’re inception stage investors, backing founders often before they’ve found their co-founder or developed their idea. We take massive pride in helping our founders identify, develop, validate, and start scaling their ideas and business models quickly through our residencies. We don’t launch and leave; instead, we keep supporting our founders throughout their company's life cycle. 
What can founders expect during the residency?
‍
Expect to move further, faster.
You’ll keep building at full sprint - just with a level of intensity, structure, and a cohort of equally committed, exceptional founders around you that makes everything move faster and hit harder.
You’ll join a cohort of 
~100 hand-picked founders
 (from a pool of ~10,000 applicants), from across the continent to join a single, highly selective residency - run from our main hub in Berlin, with a launch pad in Amsterdam.
There are two tracks, depending on your setup:
Complete teams track: 
Teams typically join for 
~6 weeks
, focused on building, tightening execution, and accelerating progress. Teams that are based in Amsterdam typically join the community physically in Berlin for 2 weeks before then working alongside our team in Amsterdam in our local launch pad.
Co-founder matching track: 
Solo founders or incomplete teams join for 
~10 weeks
, using the early phase to shape direction and founder dynamics - while continuing to build hard. If teams once completed decide to build their company from Amsterdam we will then work together on the ground from our local launch pad.
The timings are directional and if it makes sense we can also invest earlier and proceed to the next stage of building together. No matter which track you join, the expectation is the same: you're here to build something serious. Fast.
Founders can expect:
Free office space
 - Nothing moves you faster than being in-person with other highly driven builders
Full focus on building
 - No distractions. Support is bespoke and 1:1. Optional content on key topics, only when useful. This is not a startup school
Potential co-founders
 - For those looking to strengthen their team. You’ll meet a highly curated group of founders with diverse and complementary backgrounds
Resources
 - Access to the worlds best package of perks alongside A16Z & YC with a total value of 
€4M+
 for services across: infrastructure, tools, ops, legal advice and more
Community & network
 - At day zero, warm intros are everything: first hires, early customers, pivotal investors. You’ll tap into a network of exceptional peers and Antler’s own global access from day one
And the residency is only the beginning of the journey and our commitment - the real work then only starts...
Some founders arrive with traction and a team. Others show up solo and pre-idea. What matters isn’t where you start - it’s how far and how fast you're willing to go. Not everyone makes it to investment. And that’s by design.
What is Antler looking for in founders?
Antler is looking for outliers. Here’s what that means.
We back founders who are wired differently - the kind of people who 
must
 build, who don’t wait for permission, and who can turn nothing into something fast.
Some join with an MVP in motion. Others start from scratch and find a co-founder during the residency. Stage matters less than 
trajectory
 - how fast you move and how much you raise the bar.
Across backgrounds, we look for four things:
Drive
 – A level of urgency and ownership most people simply don’t have
Grit
 – Not the performative kind - the real kind, where you keep going long after it stops being fun
Spike
 – You’re 
exceptional
 at something. Could be commercial instinct, storytelling, technical depth, leadership, or attracting top talent - but it’s visible, and it makes you dangerous
Commitment
 – You’re all in. This isn’t a side project. No freelancing. No safety nets.
Our founders span a wide range. Many have 10+ years of experience and have built or led teams at scale. Some have started multiple companies before. Others are earlier in their careers but already moving like outliers - the kind of people who rally others, who ship fast, who learn faster.
‍
‍
We’re highly selective. Only ~2% of applicants are invited to join the residency. It’s not for everyone - but if you’re serious about building something massive, this is the place to start.
What are Antler's investment terms?
‍When we decide to invest, we commit €500K at inception. Those typically convert into around 10% equity at €5m valuation.
 Success for us is not writing the first check, but backing founders all the way to Series C with up to €30m. The decision to invest is made as we work together, typically within weeks - if you're building something exceptional, we move fast.
Here's how it works:
€200K are wired immediately at inception: 
€100K converts into 8.5% ownership before your next equity round, i.e. is diluted by the round
When you raise additional capital before that (typically through CLA / SAFE), our stake will dilute accordingly - no fixed equity protections apply
The other €100K is an uncapped convertible note with MFN (Most Favored Nation), converting into equity on the same terms as your next round
Up to €300K immediate follow-on commitment through our Agreement for Rolling Capital (ARC) - invested in your next round within 9 months, on the same terms as your external investors
Up to €4M in cloud, software, and AI credits via our global partner network. Companies typically use >€400K worth of free credits, which means serious extra resources at zero dilution.
Why we designed the structure this way:
Protect your ownership 
Most of the capital comes in at your next round's valuation so your dilution stays in check as you raise more
Boost your raise
 ARC acts as built-in investor validation - a pre-committed €300K that helps you close your round faster 
Increase your odds of success
 Startups that raise >€250K early are 3x more likely to raise a Series A. We help make that happen without wasting time on a fragmented angel round
Back you for the long haul
 €500K is just the start - Antler can invest up to €30M per company, supporting you from Inception through Series C
We're here to back the companies that earn it - early, seriously, and for the long run.
How does Antler work with founders once invested?
We’re usually the 
first investor on the cap table
 - and that creates a different kind of relationship.
We don’t take board seats. We don’t interfere. What we offer is trust, sparring, and long-term alignment - the kind that often makes us the first person a founder calls when it really matters.
That might be:
When co-founder dynamics get complicated
When you’re preparing for a high-stakes board discussion
When there's a term sheet on the table and you want a clear, founder-aligned perspective - not from someone further up the waterfall, but from someone who’s known you since day zero
Because we came in first, we often know the founder better than anyone else on the cap table. That creates a level of openness and support that's hard to replicate later on.
Here's how we support founders post-investment:
Strategic sparring you can trust
 - thoughtful, fast, and often just a WhatsApp away
Fundraising support that moves the needle
 - from round strategy and investor targeting to warm intros, portfolio showcases, and active guidance throughout the process
A founder network with real utility
 - 1500+ companies around the world means first customers, hiring referrals, investor backchannels, and operator connections across markets
Built-in global reach
 - whether it’s go-to-market support in Asia, intros to growth capital in the US, or a fellow deeptech founder in Australia - it’s all within the Antler network
Capital that grows with you
 - we can invest up to 
€30M per company
, from Day Zero through Series C
Free office access post-investment
 - many founders choose to stay in our offices in 
Berlin or Amsterdam 
for 3–9 months after the initial investment, working side by side with the team and other portfolio founders
World-class perks and platforms
 - including hundreds of thousands in cloud credits and best-in-class deals with top AI and infrastructure providers. [See FAQ on perks ➝]
We also create high-leverage moments for our portfolio - not on a fixed schedule, but based on real founder needs:
A week in San Francisco with curated Series A companies from around the world, preparing and meeting with top-tier Bay Area investors
A multi-day retreat in Oxford with our top European portfolio founders, culminating in a partner-level investor summit with the majority of Europe's VC capital in the room
Our role evolves, but we stay in the picture - as sparring partners, co-investors, and long-term allies.
How does Antler make investment decisions?
We invest at the end of the residency - after working closely with founders for 6–10 weeks. That gives us a rare vantage point: we make founder-first investment decisions, based on direct observation, not just pitch decks.
By that point, we’ve seen how you work, how you think, and how you lead. That’s what we base our conviction on - not one meeting, but the full experience of building alongside you.
What we look for in founders
Because we’ve worked with you day to day, we look for real, observable signals - not polish.
Outlier behavior
High energy, fast learning, sharp thinking, and a work ethic that sets the pace for everyone around you
Trajectory
It’s not about where you are now, but how fast you’re moving. Momentum - the slope of the curve - is the best predictor of long-term success
Founder–idea fit
You’re building the right company for 
you
 - based on your insight, credibility, and drive
There are hundreds of teams around the world working on similar problems, often with equally impressive backgrounds. We’re looking for indicators that this is the team that can win.
You don’t need perfection. You need pace.
What we look for in companies
We back companies with a credible path to 
billion-euro outcomes
.
We’re truly sector-agnostic - from 
satellites measuring emissions from space
 to 
consumer apps used by millions
. What matters is scale and ambition.
We look for:
Scalability
 - can this become a category leader?
Early signs of convergence
 - signals that the team is honing in on a real, valuable problem
How we decide
At the end of the residency, every team pitches to Antler’s investment committee.
The decision is made by the general partners - the same people who’ve worked with you throughout the process.
It’s fast, direct, and based on real conviction.
Build faster with 
€4M+ in perks
The tools you need to build faster and cheaper. €4M+ in credits across cloud, AI, and infrastructure — available from the moment we back you."
